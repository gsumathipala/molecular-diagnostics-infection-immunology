# Molecular Diagnostics in Infection and Immunology

A free, open-access learning resource on molecular biology and molecular diagnostics for trainees in infectious diseases, medical microbiology (bacteriology, mycology and parasitology), virology and immunology.

**Author:** Saranga Sumathipala · **Edition:** First edition, October 2026 · **Format:** PDF, 70 pages

**[Download the PDF](Molecular_Diagnostics_Infection_Immunology.pdf)** · **[Web page](https://gsumathipala.github.io/molecular-diagnostics-infection-immunology/)**

## Contents

**Part A — 18 units:** nucleic acids and pathogen genomes; laboratory organisation and biosafety; specimens and pre-analytics; extraction; PCR and RT-PCR; real-time PCR; viral load testing; syndromic panels, point-of-care testing and digital PCR; amplification methods beyond PCR, LAMP and CRISPR; antimicrobial resistance detection; sequencing technologies; pathogen bioinformatics; clinical metagenomics and 16S/ITS sequencing; genomic epidemiology; molecular mycology and parasitology; molecular immunology; quality and validation; reporting.

Also included: 26 original figures, exercises, 15 case studies, self-assessment questions and a glossary.

**Part B:** a self-study reading list of free, open-access papers, guidelines, courses and tools mapped to each unit.
